The website for *Static Site Generators in the Age of AI* is now online. It
holds the complete draft of the book: nineteen chapters that build one small
website from its first Markdown page through publication, review, and
long-term maintenance.

Three pages will help you find your way in:

- [The book](../../book/), in chapter order.
- [Learning paths](../../paths/), which suggest where to start depending on
  what you already know.
- [Learning topics](../../topics/), the thirty-three topics the book teaches.

The book is a working draft. Each chapter opens with a note recording how far
it has been tested, and those notes will change as the chapters are tried with
readers. Lecture slides and a hands-on playground are in preparation.

This site is built with Hugo and the OINK theme, and it is published to GitHub
Pages by the same kind of workflow the book teaches in Chapter 7.
